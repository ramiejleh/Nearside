How Nearside works

The Mac starts a small helper on your phone over Android's wireless debugging (or a USB cable) and talks to it directly. Nothing goes through a server.

Your Mac

Nearside appMenu bar, clipboard history, notifications, Messages, phone-app windows
Built-in adb clientPairs once, then pins your phone's key. Nothing else to install
Picture and soundVideoToolbox decodes, Metal draws, AudioToolbox plays
Clipboardboth ways
Notifications and repliesdismiss on either
Text messagesread and send SMS
Fileseither way, or into an app
App windowspicture and sound
Touch, trackpad, keysto the phone
Wi-Fi with TLS 1.3, pinned to your phone, or a USB cable. Each side proves itself before anything is sent.

Your phone

HelperRuns with adb shell rights, like scrcpy. No root
Virtual displaysOne per window, encoded by the phone's hardware. Works while locked
Nearside appNotifications, SMS, Send to Mac
No cloud. No account, server or telemetry. Your keys. A per-Mac secret that never crosses the network. Your call. Touch ID before phone apps show.
